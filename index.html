01 — About

Your books, rebuilt by someone trained to find what's wrong.

CA Inter means I've cleared 12 advanced-level papers in accounting, auditing and taxation with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Pakistan — the same qualification path as the country's top auditors. I bring that forensic, audit-minded discipline to every QuickBooks file I touch.

Most bookkeeping messes aren't one mistake — they're layers of them: a Chart of Accounts that never matched the business, months of unreconciled bank feeds, personal and business expenses tangled together, Stripe / Square / PayPal entries double-counted. I peel those layers back, period by period, until the file is clean.

And when I'm done, you don't have to take my word for it: every cleanup ends with a reconciliation report showing exactly what was fixed, so you — or your CPA — can verify every line.
